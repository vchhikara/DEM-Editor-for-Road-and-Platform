about=Create terrain-following roads and flat platforms in a new DEM. Draw a centerline, use a selected line, or use a polygon with an automatically suggested centerline. Review the corridor before editing.
    Road profiles include terrain following, grade control and a median-based terrain fit (not a volume balance). The road is designed to be level across its width, with cut and fill outside the corridor; grid resolution and bends can affect the result. Inspect results carefully on curves, steep slopes and complex polygons.
    The plugin reports DEM resolution, can create a finer interpolated working grid, and saves an edited raster, corridor, road centerline and plain-language cut/fill report. Optional difference raster and road sample points help review the result. Interpolation does not recover missing terrain detail.
    Requires QGIS 3.34–3.99, GDAL, NumPy and SciPy. Licensed under GNU GPL version 3 or later. Use projected metre-based project and DEM coordinate systems, and DEM elevations in metres for grade and slope settings. Results are preliminary terrain models and require independent engineering review. See HELP.md for operating steps and limitations.
