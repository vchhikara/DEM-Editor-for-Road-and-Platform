about=DEM Editor for Roads and Platforms helps you reshape a digital elevation model (DEM) in QGIS. You can use it to create a road corridor that follows the terrain or flatten an area to a chosen elevation. The plugin saves the result as a new GeoTIFF and leaves your original DEM unchanged.
    In Road mode, draw a centerline and set a buffer distance on the left, right, or both sides. The plugin creates a surface that is flat across the road and follows a smoothed elevation profile along its length. You can adjust the profile smoothing to control how the road follows changes in terrain.
    In Flat corridor mode, you can set the whole area to an elevation you enter or use the mean or median elevation along its edge. Define the area by drawing a buffered line, selecting polygon features, or using all features in a polygon layer.
    An optional outside transition blends the edited surface into the surrounding ground. The plugin also saves a separate polygon shapefile of the corridor and reports the number of changed pixels and the maximum cut and fill.
    Before you start: Use a file-based, single-band DEM. Both the DEM and the QGIS project need projected coordinate systems with metre units. Choose a DEM resolution suited to the width you want to represent.
    When you buffer both sides of a line, the distance applies to each side. For example, a 5 m buffer on both sides creates a road approximately 10 m wide.
    The output retains the source DEM resolution and data type; elevations in an integer DEM are rounded. The outside transition is a smooth visual blend, so review the result separately for engineering design. The plugin requires GDAL, NumPy and SciPy in the QGIS Python environment.
